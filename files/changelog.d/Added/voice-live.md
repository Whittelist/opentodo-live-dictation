- **Live dictation in quick add.** When the server's speech service is OpenAI's API, the microphone stays open and every phrase becomes a draft task as you pause; a spoken correction ("no, at half past three and with Marcos") changes the draft it is about instead of adding another, and **Add** or saying "that's all" saves the drafts as one batch with one Undo. Audio goes over WebRTC from the browser to OpenAI's Realtime API, which only detects the end of each phrase and transcribes it; each phrase is read by a model with strict structured output and checked by the server. Six optional `OPENTODO_VOICE_*` variables pick the models ([Live dictation](docs/voice-live.md)).
